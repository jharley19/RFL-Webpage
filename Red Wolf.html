Biography

The Goofy God entered the league in 2018 and immediately established themselves as a legitimate contender. In their inaugural season, they surged into the playoffs as the third seed with a 10–4 record, powered by an impressive five-game winning streak to close the regular season. However, their momentum came to an abrupt halt in the postseason, where they managed only 58.86 points in a loss to eventual champion Big Ounce. They would then fall again in the third-place game, finishing their first season in fourth place.

Heading into the 2019 season, the Goofy God elected to keep superstar running back Saquon Barkley, hoping to build on their promising debut. Much like the year before, the season began unevenly before catching fire late. A six-game winning streak to end the regular season propelled them to another third-place finish at 9–5. Once again, however, postseason success proved elusive. The Goofy God narrowly lost to the Maryland Marijuanas in the semifinals and then fell in the third-place game, finishing fourth for the second consecutive season.

Entering the 2020 season, the Goofy God had earned an unfortunate reputation as the league’s version of Marvin Lewis, a team capable of consistently reaching the playoffs but unable to win when it mattered most. Frustration mounted, and the owner even contemplated quitting the league altogether. After an illegal amount of alcohol was consumed, that thought was abandoned in favor of a declaration that he was “not fucking leaving.” Embracing a new mindset, the franchise rebranded as The Red Wolf of Wall Street. While the cultural shift produced a different outcome, it was not necessarily a better one. Injuries to key draft picks plagued the roster throughout the season, and the team struggled to find consistency. The Goofy God narrowly avoided the Panzerkampfwagen Bowl, finishing the regular season 5–9 in seventh place. In the postseason, however, they finally found success of a different kind, winning the consolation bracket and securing the first overall pick in the 2021 draft.

As the 2021 season approached, the Goofy God sought yet another fresh start, this time looking for a name and identity that could truly stand the test of time. After deep reflection in a Buffalo Wild Wings parking lot at 1:00 a.m., following the season-opening Hall of Fame Game, the franchise officially adopted the name Goofy God. The name carried multiple meanings: a reference to rejecting a “bad bitch” for being “too goofy,” a nod to the owner’s favorite rapper Eminem, who famously calls himself the “Rap God,” and a tribute to the team’s initials, “GG,” mirroring the owner’s habit of saying “GG” in the group chat before games are even finished. With a permanent identity finally in place, the Goofy God entered a new chapter of their RFL story. Holding the first overall pick, they made a risky selection by drafting Christian McCaffrey, who was coming off an injury-plagued season in which he appeared in just three games. Unfortunately, the gamble did not pay off. McCaffrey played only seven games all season, and the Goofy God struggled to stay competitive, finishing the regular season 6–9 in 10th place. This placed them in their first-ever Panzerkampfwagen Bowl, facing the dreaded punishment of attending a Washington Mystics WNBA game with no phone and no alcohol. Matched up against The Red Army, the Goofy God held a lead after the first week but collapsed in the second, thanks to poor performances from Tyler Huntley and Justin Jackson, finishing last for the first time in franchise history.

Entering the 2022 season, the Goofy God was determined to rebound from their embarrasing last place finish. However, early mistakes once again doomed the season. Selecting Najee Harris and Leonard Fournette with their first two picks proved costly, and a disastrous Week 6 trade sending away Harris and T.J. Hockenson in exchange for Curtis Samuel and Hayden Hurst effectively sealed their fate. Through ten weeks, the Goofy God had managed just two wins and found themselves back in last place. By rivalry week in Week 15, they were 4–10 and had clinched another Panzerkampfwagen Bowl appearance. Before that, they suffered one final blowout loss to rival The Hurts Helicopter, driven by dud performances from Justin Herbert and Bam Knight. In the Panzerkampfwagen Bowl, the Goofy God faced Gruden’s Plan and managed to survive, thanks to a strong first week showing fueled by Fournette, Keenan Allen, and Devin Singletary. They narrowly avoided punishment and finished the season in ninth place.

The 2023 season marked a turning point for the Goofy God. After two consecutive Panzerkampfwagen Bowl appearances, the franchise was determined to reestablish itself as a legitimate contender. A much stronger draft, paired with a blockbuster pre–Week 1 trade that brought in Alvin Kamara and A.J. Brown in exchange for Nick Chubb and Michael Pittman, laid the foundation for success. The move paid immediate dividends, as the Goofy God won seven of their first ten games. By rivalry week in Week 15, they had clinched a playoff spot and sat at 10–4. In the second edition of their bowl matchup against The Hurts Helicopter, the Goofy God emerged victorious behind strong performances from Chris Godwin and Deebo Samuel. Seeking their first playoff win in franchise history, they faced the Mullet Man in the semifinals. Unfortunately, disappointing games from Kamara and Deebo resulted in a narrow loss, ending their season in third place.

The 2024 season saw the Goofy God attempt to build on their resurgence and finally break through to championship glory. Another strong draft helped them get off to a fast start, and by Week 10 they sat in first place at 6–4, mirroring their position from the year before. By rivalry week in Week 15, the Goofy God stood 8–6 in second place, once again battling for a playoff spot. In their bowl matchup against The Hurts Helicopter, they squeaked out another victory thanks to big performances from Jalen Hurts and A.J. Brown, vaulting them into first place and back into the playoffs. Despite entering the postseason with an 0–3 all-time playoff record, hopes were high that this would finally be the breakthrough year. Instead, the curse continued. In the semifinals against Big Ounce, dud performances from Hurts, Brian Robinson, and the Cardinals defense led to another crushing loss, and the Goofy God finished third once again.

Entering the 2025 season, after back-to-back third-place finishes, the Goofy God was desperate to finally win a playoff game. Their draft was uneven from the start. First-round pick Malik Nabers appeared in only four games before tearing his ACL, and quarterback Jayden Daniels battled injuries throughout the year. However, the team struck gold in the second round by selecting Christian McCaffrey, who delivered a monster RB1 season. By rivalry week in Week 15, the Goofy God was 8–6 in fifth place and needed a win to secure a playoff berth. In a must-win bowl matchup against The Hurts Helicopter, they fell short for the first time since 2022, undone by poor performances from Sam Darnold and Michael Pittman. The loss eliminated them from playoff contention and sent them to their first Toes Bowl since 2020. They would lose to Big Ounce in the first round and then to Wack Attacks in the second, finishing the season in eighth place.
